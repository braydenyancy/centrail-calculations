# Code of conduct

Everyone should be able to examine and improve this project without harassment.
These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, reviews and other
project-managed spaces, including maintainers' participation.

Challenge calculations, assumptions and evidence directly. Explain disagreements
with examples and sources, consider corrections, and give other people room to
learn. Treat participants with respect regardless of identity, background,
experience or ability.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, deliberate personal
humiliation and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.
Repeated disruption and retaliation against someone reporting a concern are also
unacceptable. Technical disagreement, including criticism of a method, is welcome.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content, close a discussion, limit
participation or block a participant. They should consider context, apply these
standards consistently and explain moderation decisions when doing so does not
expose private information. Participants may ask for reconsideration with relevant
context; moderation is not a mechanism for suppressing evidence-based criticism.

For a conduct concern, contact the repository owner using a contact method they
publish on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/braydenyancy). If no suitable
private channel is available, use GitHub's
[abuse-reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Do not post private details in an issue. If the concern involves a maintainer,
GitHub's reporting process is an independent option for conduct on its platform.
